China HSR Simulation Progress Log
=================================

Project overview:
This project implements a realistic China high-speed rail simulation using local station, train-route, and HOTOSM railway data. The most important behavior is segment-aware booking: seats are occupied only over the station interval a passenger travels and become available for later non-overlapping passengers after alighting.
